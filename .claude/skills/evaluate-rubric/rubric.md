# Hackathon judging rubric

The general judging rubric for the preliminary and final rounds, as given to the team. The problem statement (`docs/challenge.md`) adds what to consider for each criterion.

| Criterion | Judges | Weight | What they look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impact and problem solving | Non-profit partner | 30% | Potential to make an impact on the chosen problem statement |
| Technical execution (live demo) | Technical | 35% | The solution working, live, in the demo |
| Innovation | Technical | 20% | Use of new technology, algorithms or methods |
| Demo and storytelling | All | 15% | How clearly the demo tells the story |
| Accessibility | All | Bonus | 1. Did the team use an accessibility tool, such as axe or GitHub checks, on their solution? 2. A demo of keyboard use (Tab focus) |

Notes for scoring:
- 55% of the score comes from technical judges, and 35% is the **live** demo. A feature that can't be shown working live earns little under technical execution, however good the code.
- The non-profit partner judges weigh impact. They ask: would BRIDGEGOOD use this, and does it answer the brief's problem, not a neighbouring one?
- The pitch is 4 minutes with 1 minute of Q&A (see `docs/demo-script.md`).
