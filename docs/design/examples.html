Bridge Studio

Design that shows who’s connected and what’s still a draft

A static showcase of Bridge Studio, the BridgeGoodx design system, as it’s built in the app. It opens straight from the repository, with no server or login. The spec is DESIGN.md, and the running app’s /design page shows the real components.

Body text meets AAA contrast. Every role carries an icon and a label. AI output always looks like a draft until a person edits or accepts it. The Design for Social Good philosophy belongs to BRIDGEGOOD, and a BRIDGEGOOD steward decides what changes in it.
